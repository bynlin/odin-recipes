Rustic Potato Salad

This dump-and-mix potato salad is inspired by Japanese potato salad, with everything added and mixed right in one bowl. Tender potatoes, jammy eggs, ham, cucumber, and shallot come together in a creamy Kewpie mayonnaise dressing for an easy, satisfying side dish.

Ingredients

  • 5 tablespoons Greek yogurt
  • 4 tablespoons milk
  • 3 tablespoons Japanese mayonnaise (such as Kewpie)
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 4 medium potatoes, washed and scrubbed
  • 5 large eggs, divided
  • 1 Persian cucumber, sliced
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 1/2 cup diced ham

Steps

  1. Stir together Greek yogurt, milk, Japanese mayonnaise, salt and pepper in a salad bowl for the dressing; set aside.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and cook until tender, 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, bring a second pot of water to a rolling boil. Place eggs on a spoon and carefully lower into the water. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 6 minutes and 30 seconds so they stay jammy.
  4. Fill a bowl with cold water and ice cubes. Remove eggs from the boiling water using a slotted spoon and submerge in the ice bath for 3 minutes.
  5. Peel potatoes and eggs.
  6. Add potatoes, 4 eggs, cucumber, ham, and shallot to the bowl with the dressing. Break potatoes and eggs apart with a wooden spoon and mix with the dressing.
  7. Cut remaining jammy egg in half and add as garnish on top of the potato salad.
