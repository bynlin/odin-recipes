Bak Kut Teh

  1. Rub salt all over bak kut then rinse the salt off
  2. Put bak kut into a pot of water. Bring to a boil. Let it boil for 2 to 3 mins. Drain and 'wash' ribs.
  3. Put a lot of washed, unpeeled garlic cloves together with soup packet in pot of water, bring these to a boil
  4. Add around a teaspoonful of dark soy sauce
  5. Add clean ribs into boiling soup
  6. Turn fire down to medium low for 30 mins, to 45 mins, to 1 hour

Chicken Rice

  1. Rub salt all over the chicken, leave it for 5-10 mins, rinse off the salt
  2. Put lots of ginger and pandan leaves in water and bring to a boil
  3. Put chicken, stuffed with ginger and pandan leaves, into boiling water, cover for 30 mins, medium to medium high heat
  4. Use chopstick to poke chicken, if it slides through thoroughly, then switch fire off and cover for 10 mins.
  5. If not, cover and continue boiling for 10 mins more.
  6. Wash rice then mix in minced ginger, garlic and pandan leaf.
  7. After 10 mins of keeping cooked chicken in covered pot with no fire, take out chicken and rub it with sesame oil
  8. Taste and season soup to use it to cook rice.
  9. Add cabbage, fishballs into soup and cook it, then done.
